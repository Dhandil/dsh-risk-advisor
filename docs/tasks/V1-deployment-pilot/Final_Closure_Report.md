## 4. Human real-Browser observations

The Pilot Freeze allows completion when some frozen scenarios cannot naturally trigger, provided at least four useful real observations exist.

The final Pilot contains more than four materially useful human observations from the real profile:

1. **Native Approval authority observation**  
   A genuine outside-workspace escalation produced the real Native Approval surface. Reject prevented the side effect.

2. **Browser bridge READY observation**  
   After the Browser bridge maintenance repair, Risk Advisor transitioned from permanent `UNAVAILABLE` to READY in the real profile while Native Approval remained authoritative.

3. **Original READY usability observation**  
   The user found the full READY card too long, visually dense, and lacking clear hierarchy.

4. **Card Hierarchy rejection observation**  
   The intermediate smaller-card candidate remained too large and annoying for repeated approvals. The user rejected that product form before Full/publication.

5. **Compact Risk Indicator acceptance observation**  
   The final compact indicator occupied approximately one row, kept hazard/recommendation/reason scannable, preserved `danger-full-access` and DEGRADED, and moved full analysis to a user-triggered Modal.

6. **Modal round-trip observation**  
   Opening and closing detailed analysis did not replace or settle the pending Native Approval and did not create duplicate/stale advisory state.

These observations satisfy the human-usability purpose of Stage B without fabricating ratings for the frozen scenarios that did not naturally occur.
